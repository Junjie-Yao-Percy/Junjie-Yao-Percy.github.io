About

I'm a first-year master's student at UESTC (电子科技大学) majoring in Computer Science & Technology, working at the intersection of deep learning, computer vision, and efficient model deployment.

My research spans neural architecture search (NAS), attention mechanisms, causal representation learning, and inference optimization on Ascend NPUs. I care about models that don't just hit benchmark numbers, but actually ship — to UAVs, edge devices, and autonomous systems.

Currently collaborating with Guangming Lab (Shenzhen) on vLLM adaptation to Ascend NPUs, and with Hetao Institute on embodied-AI simulation for infrastructure maintenance.
